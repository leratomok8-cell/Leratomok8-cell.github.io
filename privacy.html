Privacy Policy

Yebo Bookings (South Africa) · Last updated: 23 November 2026

Yebo Bookings provides a WhatsApp and email booking assistant for restaurants, pubs and clubs ("venues"). This page explains what information the assistant handles, why, and what your rights are. We follow South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Who is responsible for your information

When you message a venue's WhatsApp number to make a booking, the venue decides why your information is used (to handle your booking). Yebo Bookings runs the assistant on the venue's behalf and only uses your information for that purpose.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and the name on your WhatsApp profile.
  • The messages you send to the assistant, and the details of your booking: event type, date, number of guests, meal and drinks choices, seating section, quotation and deposit status.
  • Whether you confirm, change or cancel a booking, including your replies to booking reminders.

We do not download or store the pictures or documents you send, such as proof of payment. The assistant only notes that proof was received.

Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry, prepare a quotation and hold your date.
  • To confirm your booking and send you reminders before the event.
  • To let the venue's staff see and manage bookings, and alert them when something needs their attention.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • The venue you are booking with and its authorised staff.
  • Service providers that help us run the assistant: WhatsApp (Meta) delivers the messages, and our hosting and database providers store the booking details. Some of these providers keep data on servers outside South Africa.

How long we keep it

The assistant remembers a conversation for up to 60 days. Booking records are kept while the venue needs them to run its bookings, and are deleted when you ask or when the venue stops using the service.

Your rights

You may ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, and you may object to its use. Email us at the address below and we will respond promptly. If you are unhappy, you may complain to South Africa's Information Regulator.

Security

Access to booking information is protected, and only the venue and our team can see it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

The service is not meant for people under 18 making bookings on their own.
